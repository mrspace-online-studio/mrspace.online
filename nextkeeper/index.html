Adoption

Three steps to take one home.

  1. 01

    Read the file

    Every resident has an intake file: where it came from, what it went through, what it needs. The measurements and condition are checked by hand.

  2. 02

    Adopt

    Pay the adoption fee. The piece is taken off the hanger the moment it is yours. No one else can claim it.

  3. 03

    Take it home

    It leaves with its numbered adoption certificate. Scan the tag and its story comes with it, wherever it goes next.

House rules

Clothes aren't meant to wait.

  • I. Every resident is one of a kind. When it is adopted, it is gone.
  • II. The intake file tells you the truth about size, cloth and condition.
  • III. The story is how we remember it. Every story is true. Some more than others.
  • IV. The longest residents are seen first and their adoption fee gets lower.
