How adoption works

Three steps. One piece. A new chapter.

  1. 01Read the file. Size, cloth and condition are checked by hand. The story is how we remember it.
  2. 02Adopt. Pay the adoption fee and it comes off the hanger for good.
  3. 03Take it home. It ships with a numbered certificate. Scan the tag and its story follows.
